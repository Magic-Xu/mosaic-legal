SnapMosaic Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 3, 2026

Welcome to SnapMosaic ("the App"). SnapMosaic is a local-first privacy editor for photos and videos. It provides mosaic, blur, block masking, marker highlighting, user-added text watermarks, face emoji stickers, and automatic video face masking. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled.

1. Information We Access or Process

We do not require account registration and do not operate our own account or cloud storage service.

  • Photos and videos you choose through Android, or share into the App from another app, are processed locally on your device.
  • Photo edits (including strokes, shapes, effects, marker highlighting, user-added text watermarks, and face emoji stickers) and video face detection, tracking, and masking data are used locally to render results. User-entered watermark text is kept for the current editing session and is included in an output only when you save or share that output.
  • App settings such as language, export format, resolution, metadata-removal preference, recent editor tools, effects, colors, strengths, sizes, and face-sticker defaults may be stored in the App's private local storage.
  • Pro entitlement status and the expiry time of rewarded SnapMosaic-brand-watermark-free access are stored locally. Google Play processes purchases and returns product and active purchase status to the App; we do not receive or store payment card details, order numbers, or purchase tokens.
  • Processed video drafts may be stored temporarily in the App's private cache until you save, replace, close, cancel, or encounter a processing failure.
  • Your selected photo and video content is not uploaded to our servers.
  • If you choose “Report a problem,” the App creates a local diagnostic text file containing random report/session IDs, creation time, app and Android versions, API level, device manufacturer/model, locale, install source, current screen, media type, processing stage and operation identifier, stable outcome/reason, and up to 250 recent sanitized operational log entries. A payment report may also contain Google Play Billing response codes and names, product ID, purchase-state counts, and projected Pro status. Reports exclude selected media, URIs, file paths, file names, recognized text, user-entered text, account details, payment credentials, order numbers, and purchase tokens.
  • When ads, analytics, crash monitoring, or on-device ML features are enabled, third-party SDKs may automatically collect data such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app/ad interactions, app usage events, crash logs, diagnostics, SDK usage, and model download status as described below.

2. Permissions and Purpose

The App may request system capabilities only for core features:

  • Photos/Media access: select photos or videos from the system picker/gallery.
  • Media write access: save edited photos or processed videos to your local album.
  • System share: receive an image you explicitly share into SnapMosaic, share output media, or hand a diagnostic attachment to another app when you explicitly choose to do so.

These permissions are used on demand and not for background data collection.

3. Data Processing

  • Photo editing and video processing are performed locally.
  • Smart detection for faces, QR/barcodes, and sensitive text suggestions in photos is performed on device using Google ML Kit models.
  • Automatic video face detection and tracking is performed on device using Google ML Kit models; video decoding, masking, and export are performed on device.
  • Saved output is written to your device media library.
  • We do not send your photo or video content externally without your explicit sharing action.
  • A diagnostic report stays in the App cache and is not sent automatically. The App first tries to open a compatible email app with the support address, subject, message, and attachment prepared. If no email app is available or launch fails, the Android system share sheet opens with the subject, message, and attachment, and you choose the receiving app. Only the receiving external app gets temporary read access to the attachment and handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. You can review, send/share, or cancel. Creating a new report removes older report files from the App cache.
  • If privacy enhancement is enabled for photo export, the App attempts to remove metadata such as EXIF/GPS information when saving output.

5. Security, Retention, and Deletion

  • Local settings, cached entitlements, and temporary files are kept in App-private storage. Android backup is disabled for the App. Network traffic handled by the listed Google SDKs uses their documented encrypted transport.
  • Settings and cached Pro status remain until overwritten, you clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. Rewarded access expires at its displayed expiry time.
  • User-entered watermark text is limited to the current editing session. Saved outputs remain in your media library until you delete them.
  • Unsaved video drafts are removed when you close or replace the current media, cancel processing, or processing fails, and stale cache files are cleaned periodically.
  • The App keeps only the most recently prepared diagnostic report in its diagnostic cache; creating a new report deletes the previous one. Android may clear cached files, and clearing App data or uninstalling removes App-private copies. Once you send or share a report, the receiving app or service controls its copy and retention.
  • Third-party SDK data is retained and deleted under the applicable provider's policies and controls. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Children

The App is intended for general users. If you are a minor, please use the App under guardian guidance.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • You may delete locally saved outputs, clear the App's local data, or uninstall the App at any time.
  • You may revoke media permissions via system settings.
  • You may reset or delete your advertising ID in Android system settings where supported.
  • If a consent form is shown in your region, you may update your consent choices through the privacy options provided by the App or Google form when available.
  • You may review purchases and request eligible refunds through Google Play. The App also provides a restore-purchase action for the current Google Play account.
  • You may contact us with privacy questions or requests. Because SnapMosaic has no developer-operated account or cloud storage, we generally cannot identify or delete local-only data on your behalf; use the device controls described above. Requests about data held by a third-party service are subject to that provider's process.
