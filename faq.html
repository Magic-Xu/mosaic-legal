Is SnapMosaic free to use?

Photo editing tools are free to use, and free exports include a SnapMosaic brand watermark by default. An optional one-time Pro purchase removes that brand watermark and in-app ads.

Are my photos uploaded for editing?

No account is required. Your selected photos are processed on your device and are not uploaded to SnapMosaic servers for editing. Ads, purchases and supporting services may use a network connection; see the privacy policy for details.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Use the Google Play account you purchased with, then open the Pro page in SnapMosaic settings and choose Restore purchases. An active purchase removes the SnapMosaic brand watermark and in-app ads.

Why is some content only outlined?

Detection can miss details. An outlined area is only a suggestion; tap it to cover it. If detection is unavailable, use manual masking or open detection help from the question-mark menu.

How do I hide just part of a line?

Long-press a detected line of text, then choose the individual words or segments to hide. For anything missed, open the masking tool, choose an effect, and use a rectangle or brush from “Adjust”. Use Undo if you make a mistake.

Does sharing also save a copy?

“Save a copy” writes a new image to your gallery. “Share” opens the Android share sheet and does not also save it to the gallery. Your original photo is not overwritten.
