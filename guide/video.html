Feature Lab

Video masking · Experimental

Automatically cover faces in a video, with processing on your device.

How to use it

Open Feature Lab in Settings to use automatic video masking. Choose a video of up to 10 minutes, then start automatic masking. Review the preview and scrub through the video before saving. After saving, you can share the result.

The output is MP4 and preserves the frame proportions and audio. This feature currently offers automatic face masking only: there are no manual mask adjustments, text recognition or video editing tools. If a face is missed, do not share that result without further processing.
