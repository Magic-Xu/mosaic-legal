Getting started

Quick start

Choose an image, cover private details, and export a copy. Follow these four steps in SnapMosaic for Android.

Before you start

Install SnapMosaic from Google Play. You can use the sample photo to practise, without opening your own photos. No account or Pro purchase is required.

01Choose a photo

Open SnapMosaic and choose a photo from the system photo picker. For a first try, use “Try a sample photo” on the start screen.

You can select up to 10 images. Each image keeps its own edits; review them individually before exporting the group.

02Smart detection

Wait for the photo to load. If automatic masking is enabled, review the applied masks. Otherwise, tap “Smart” at the bottom to apply suggested masks to faces, codes, and common sensitive text.

Detection can miss details. An outlined area is only a suggestion; tap it to cover it. If detection is unavailable, use manual masking or open detection help from the question-mark menu.

03Manual masking and word selection

Long-press a detected line of text, then choose the individual words or segments to hide. For anything missed, open the masking tool, choose an effect, and use a rectangle or brush from “Adjust”. Use Undo if you make a mistake.

For sensitive text, use a solid block at 100% opacity and cover the whole detail. The marker highlights content and leaves it readable.

04Save and share

Tap the share icon in the top toolbar. Choose JPEG, PNG, or WebP and an output size. Enable “Remove metadata (EXIF/GPS)” if you want to remove those details from the exported file.

“Save a copy” writes a new image to your gallery. “Share” opens the Android share sheet and does not also save it to the gallery. Your original photo is not overwritten.

One last look before you share

  • Check faces, names, phone numbers, addresses, and codes at a readable zoom level.
  • Make sure each detail you want to hide is fully covered. An outline alone does not hide anything in the export.
  • Open the saved image and check the result before sending it.
